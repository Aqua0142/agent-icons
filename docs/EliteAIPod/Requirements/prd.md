## Project Summary

This project aims to build a simple, two-page furniture shopping cart application. The first page will display a list of products (Chairs, Tables, Designer frames) allowing users to add them to a cart. The second page will display the cart's contents, the total price, and allow for modifications like removing items or changing quantities. The cart state will persist for the user's browser session.

## Business Context

The provided business domain research was used to refine the user stories and non-functional requirements to align with modern e-commerce best practices, even for a simple application. Insights on competitor features informed the need for clear, immediate user feedback upon adding an item to the cart (US-002 ACs). The common user journey analysis directly mapped to the two-page flow, validating the core requirements. Accessibility considerations from the research were translated into a dedicated user story (US-012) and a high-priority NFR, specifying WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the target. Performance benchmarks influenced the NFR for page load times. UX patterns for cart management, such as easy editing and transparent pricing, were incorporated into the acceptance criteria for the cart-related stories. Features mentioned in the research like AR, AI recommendations, and user accounts were noted but deliberately excluded to adhere to the provided scope.

## User Stories

### US-001 — View Product List

**As a** shopper
**I want** to see a list of available furniture products on the main page
**So that** I can browse the items for sale.

- **Epic:** Product Discovery & Selection
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the product list page
- When the page loads
- Then I should see product listings under the categories 'Chairs', 'Tables', and 'Designer frames'.
- And each product listing must display an image, name, price, and an 'Add to Cart' button.

### US-002 — Add Item to Cart

**As a** shopper
**I want** to add a product to my shopping cart from the list page
**So that** I can select it for purchase.

- **Epic:** Product Discovery & Selection
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the product list page
- When I click the 'Add to Cart' button for a product
- Then the item is added to my cart.
- And I receive immediate visual feedback that the item was added (e.g., the cart icon updates its item count).

### US-003 — View Cart Contents

**As a** shopper
**I want** to view the contents of my shopping cart on a separate page
**So that** I can review my selections and see the total price.

- **Epic:** Cart Management
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 2

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I have added items to my cart
- When I navigate to the cart page
- Then I see a list of all items I have added, with their name, price, and quantity.
- And the page displays a calculated total price for all items in the cart.

### US-004 — Remove Item from Cart

**As a** shopper
**I want** to remove an individual item from my cart
**So that** I can change my mind about a purchase.

- **Epic:** Cart Management
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 2

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the cart page with items in it
- When I click the 'Remove' button for a specific item
- Then that item is removed from my cart.
- And the total price is immediately updated to reflect the removal.

### US-005 — Change Item Quantity in Cart

**As a** shopper
**I want** to change the quantity of an item in my cart
**So that** I can buy more or fewer of a single product.

- **Epic:** Cart Management
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the cart page with an item
- When I increase or decrease the quantity of that item
- Then the item's subtotal and the cart's total price are immediately updated.
- And the quantity cannot be reduced below one (item must be removed instead).

### US-006 — Clear Entire Cart

**As a** shopper
**I want** to clear my entire cart in one action
**So that** I can easily start my shopping over.

- **Epic:** Cart Management
- **Priority:** medium
- **Story Points:** 1

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I have multiple items in my cart
- When I click the 'Clear Cart' button
- Then all items are removed from the cart and the total price becomes zero.

### US-007 — Proceed to Checkout

**As a** shopper
**I want** to proceed to a checkout step from the cart page
**So that** I can complete my purchase.

- **Epic:** Cart Management
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 1

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the cart page
- When I click the 'Proceed to Checkout' button
- Then I am taken to a placeholder checkout page or an alert is shown.
- And the button is disabled if the cart is empty.

### US-008 — Navigate Back to Shopping

**As a** shopper
**I want** to navigate from the cart page back to the product list
**So that** I can add more items.

- **Epic:** Cart Management
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 1

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the cart page
- When I click a 'Continue Shopping' link or button
- Then I am navigated back to the product list page.

### US-009 — Application Navigation

**As a** user
**I want** to easily navigate between the product list and the shopping cart
**So that** I can manage my shopping experience efficiently.

- **Epic:** Core User Experience
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 2

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on any page of the application
- When I look at the header
- Then I see a persistent shopping cart icon.
- And clicking the cart icon takes me to the cart page.

### US-010 — Session Continuity

**As a** shopper
**I want** my cart contents to be saved during my browser session
**So that** I don't lose my selections if I refresh the page or open a new tab.

- **Epic:** Core User Experience
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I have added items to my cart
- When I refresh the page
- Then my cart contents remain unchanged.
- And when I open the site in a new tab, my cart contents are still present.
- And when I close the browser and reopen it, the cart is empty (session storage).

### US-011 — View Application States

**As a** user
**I want** to see clear states for content areas
**So that** I understand what is happening if products are loading, my cart is empty, or an error occurs.

- **Epic:** Core User Experience
- **Priority:** medium
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the product list page
- When products are being loaded from the server
- Then I see a loading indicator.
- Given I navigate to the cart page with no items
- When the page loads
- Then I see a message indicating my cart is empty and a link to the product page.

### US-012 — Accessible Shopping Experience

**As a** user with disabilities
**I want** to be able to use the shopping cart with assistive technologies
**So that** I can have an equitable shopping experience.

- **Epic:** Core User Experience
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 5

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am using only a keyboard
- When I am on the product list page
- Then I can tab to and activate each 'Add to Cart' button.
- Given I am using a screen reader
- When I add an item to the cart
- Then an audible announcement confirms the action (e.g., 'Chair was added to your cart').
