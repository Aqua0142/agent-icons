## Project Summary

To develop a HIPAA-compliant provider directory and appointment scheduling platform for health insurance plan members. The system will enable members to search for in-network providers, view their profiles, and book or cancel appointments online, aiming to improve self-service capabilities and member satisfaction.

## User Stories

### US-001 — Member Registration

**As a** new health plan member
**I want** to create a secure account
**So that** I can access the provider directory and schedule appointments.

- **Epic:** Member Authentication
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 5

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the registration page, when I enter my required details (e.g., member ID, name, email, password) and submit, then my account is created.
- Given I attempt to register, when my chosen password does not meet the minimum security requirements, then I am shown an error message detailing the requirements.
- Given I successfully register, then I receive a confirmation email to verify my account.

### US-002 — Member Login

**As a** registered member
**I want** to log in to the platform
**So that** I can access my personalized dashboard and features.

- **Epic:** Member Authentication
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am a registered member, when I enter my correct credentials and submit, then I am successfully logged in and redirected to my dashboard.
- Given I am a registered member, when I enter incorrect credentials, then I am shown an error message and can attempt to log in again.

### US-003 — Member Logout

**As a** logged-in member
**I want** to securely log out of my account
**So that** my session is terminated and my information is protected.

- **Epic:** Member Authentication
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 2

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am logged in, when I click the 'Logout' button, then my session is ended and I am redirected to the login page.
- Given my session has been terminated, when I use the browser's back button, then I cannot access any authenticated pages.

### US-004 — Provider Search

**As a** member
**I want** to search for providers by name, specialty, and ZIP code
**So that** I can find a list of relevant healthcare professionals.

- **Epic:** Provider Directory
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 5

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the provider search page, when I enter a valid specialty and ZIP code and click 'Search', then I see a list of matching providers.
- Given I perform a search, when no providers match my criteria, then I see a message indicating that no results were found.
- Given I see search results, then each result is displayed in a card format with the provider's name, specialty, and location.

### US-005 — View Provider Profile

**As a** member
**I want** to view a detailed profile for a selected provider
**So that** I can make an informed decision before booking.

- **Epic:** Provider Directory
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I have a list of search results, when I click on a provider's card, then I am taken to their detailed profile page.
- Given I am on a provider's profile page, then I can see their full name, specialty, office address, phone number, and a map of their location.

### US-006 — View Provider Availability

**As a** member
**I want** to view a provider's available appointment slots
**So that** I can choose a time that fits my schedule.

- **Epic:** Appointment Scheduling
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 5

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on a provider's profile page, when I click a 'View Availability' button, then I am shown a calendar view of their open appointment times.
- Given I am viewing the availability calendar, when I select a date, then I see the specific time slots available for that day.
- Given I am viewing the availability calendar, then unavailable slots are clearly marked and not selectable.

### US-007 — Book an Appointment

**As a** member
**I want** to book an available appointment slot with a provider
**So that** I can schedule my visit without making a phone call.

- **Epic:** Appointment Scheduling
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 8

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I have selected an available time slot, when I click to book, then I am taken to a confirmation screen to review the details (provider, date, time, location).
- Given I am on the confirmation screen, when I confirm the details, then the appointment is booked and the time slot becomes unavailable to others.
- Given I have booked an appointment, then I receive an email confirmation with all the appointment details.

### US-008 — View Upcoming Appointments

**As a** member
**I want** to see a list of my upcoming appointments
**So that** I can keep track of my scheduled visits.

- **Epic:** Appointment Scheduling
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am logged in, when I navigate to the 'My Appointments' section, then I see a list of all my future appointments.
- Given I am viewing my list of appointments, then each entry displays the provider's name, specialty, date, time, and location.

### US-009 — Cancel an Appointment

**As a** member
**I want** to cancel an upcoming appointment through the portal
**So that** I can manage my schedule and free up the slot for others.

- **Epic:** Appointment Scheduling
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am viewing my upcoming appointments, when I select the option to cancel an appointment, then I am asked to confirm the cancellation.
- Given I confirm the cancellation, then the appointment is removed from my list and the provider's slot becomes available again.
- Given I have cancelled an appointment, then I receive an email notification confirming the cancellation.

### US-010 — Core Feature Navigation (Discoverability)

**As a** logged-in member
**I want** to easily find and access the main features of the application
**So that** I can navigate between searching for providers and managing my appointments efficiently.

- **Epic:** User Experience & Core Functionality
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 2

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am logged in, then a persistent primary navigation bar is visible on all pages.
- Given the navigation bar is visible, then it contains clear links to 'Provider Search' and 'My Appointments'.

### US-011 — Session Continuity

**As a** logged-in member
**I want** my session to be maintained as I navigate the site
**So that** I don't have to log in again after a page refresh or opening a new tab.

- **Epic:** User Experience & Core Functionality
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am logged in, when I refresh the current page, then I remain logged in.
- Given I am logged in, when I open a link from the site in a new browser tab, then I am already authenticated in the new tab.

### US-012 — System Feedback States

**As a** member
**I want** to see clear loading, empty, and error states when interacting with the system
**So that** I understand what is happening and am not left waiting without feedback.

- **Epic:** User Experience & Core Functionality
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I initiate a provider search, when the system is fetching results, then a visible loading indicator (e.g., spinner) is displayed.
- Given I perform a search that yields no results, then a user-friendly message like 'No providers found' is displayed instead of a blank page.
- Given an unexpected error occurs during an action (e.g., booking an appointment), then a clear error message is displayed to me.

### US-013 — Basic Keyboard Accessibility

**As a** member who relies on a keyboard for navigation
**I want** to be able to navigate and interact with all core functions using my keyboard
**So that** I can use the application without needing a mouse.

- **Epic:** User Experience & Core Functionality
- **Priority:** medium
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on any page, when I press the 'Tab' key, then the focus moves sequentially through all interactive elements (links, buttons, form fields).
- Given an element has focus, when I press 'Enter' or 'Spacebar', then the element's primary action is triggered (e.g., a button is clicked).


## Non-Functional Requirements

- **security** (critical): The system must be HIPAA compliant, ensuring all Protected Health Information (PHI) is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).- **security** (critical): Access to data must be governed by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Members can only view and manage their own appointments and data.- **performance** (high): Provider search results and provider profile pages must load in under 3 seconds on a standard broadband connection.- **accessibility** (medium): The user interface must meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.- **reliability** (high): The platform must maintain a 99.9% uptime, ensuring members can access scheduling services at any time.
## Assumptions

- Members are defined as health insurance plan enrollees.
- The system must adhere to U.S. HIPAA regulations for data privacy and security.
- This is a greenfield project with no integrations to external EHR/EMR systems in this initial phase.
- An administrative interface for managing provider data (profiles, schedules, etc.) will be built but is considered out of scope for these member-facing stories.
- The initial user base is small (<10,000 members), so initial architecture can be optimized for this scale.
